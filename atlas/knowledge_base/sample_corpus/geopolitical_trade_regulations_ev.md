# Geopolitical Trade Regulations & Supply Chain Policy Impact (2025-2026)

## Global Trade Frameworks and Protectionist Policies

### 1. United States Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) - Foreign Entity of Concern (FEOC)
- Starting in 2025 and continuing into 2026, the US Treasury Section 30D tax credit requires strict compliance with Foreign Entity of Concern (FEOC) definitions.
- Direct Rule: Any EV battery containing critical minerals extracted, processed, or recycled by an entity owned or controlled by specified jurisdictions (including China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea) is entirely ineligible for the $7,500 consumer tax credit.
- Tracing Complexity: Tier-1 battery manufacturers (Panasonic, LG Energy Solution, SK On, Samsung SDI) have spent over $1.2B establishing digital provenance tracing platforms to audit mineral supply paths down to individual mine pits.

### 2. European Union Battery Regulation & Battery Passport
- The EU Battery Regulation (Regulation 2023/1542) mandates the implementation of digital "Battery Passports" across Europe by 2026.
- Every industrial and EV battery above 2 kWh must carry a tamper-proof QR code detailing:
  - Total lifecycle carbon footprint (from raw extraction to end-of-life).
  - Recycled material content ratios (minimum mandatory targets for cobalt, lead, lithium, and nickel).
  - Supply chain due diligence documentation verifying adherence to human rights and OECD conflict-free mineral guidelines.
- Non-compliant batteries are prohibited from entering or operating within the EU single market.

### 3. Critical Mineral Export Controls and Strategic Reserves
- Graphite Export Restrictions: Implementation of dual-use export license regimes for synthetic and natural graphite flakes has caused temporary spot-market price spikes of up to 40% for North American anode fabricators.
- National Stockpiling Initiatives: The US Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) and the European Critical Raw Materials Board have allocated over $4.5B in 2025/2026 toward physical strategic stockpiles of refined battery-grade lithium hydroxide and cobalt briquettes.
